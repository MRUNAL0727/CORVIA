Follow these steps to download your Daily Activity Metrics from Google Fit.
  1. 1
    Open Google Fit app Tap your profile picture in the top right corner
  2. 2
    Go to Data & Privacy Tap Fit data and privacy → Fit data permissions
    Enable Store vitals data & Store cycle tracking data
  3. 3
    Export your data Scroll up to Your Fit data → tap Export your data
  4. 4
    Configure the export Select Fit → tap Next step
    Transfer: Send download link via email · Frequency: Export once · File type: ZIP
  5. 5
    Create & download Tap Create export → Your report will arrive in 10 minutes - check your inbox → tap Manage Google Takeout request → Download
  6. 6
    Find the CSV file Open the Takeout ZIP → Fit folder → Daily activity metrics folder → select Daily activity metrics.csv
💡 The CSV contains all your step, speed, calories and heart data - CORVIA reads it automatically.
