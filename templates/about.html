About CORVIA

Where clinical science meets your daily life.

For seventy years, the American Heart Association has built the science that physicians trust. For years, Google Fit has quietly recorded the truth of your days - step by step, heart point by heart point.

CORVIA is where they finally meet.

We are students who asked: what if you could connect decades of clinical research with the data you already collect?

Your 10-year heart risk is not a verdict. It is a starting point - an honest understanding of where you stand today, so you can decide where you want to go tomorrow.

Your heart's story is written in every step you have ever taken. CORVIA is here to help you read it.

Under the Hood

How CORVIA Works

Step 01
Clinical Parameters
You enter your age, sex, total and HDL cholesterol, blood pressure, diabetes status and smoking history.
🩺
📂
Step 02
Google Fit Lifestyle Data
You upload your Google Fit Daily Activity Metrics CSV - daily steps, move minutes, calories, heart points, walking speed and weight data are automatically extracted.
Step 03
Lifestyle Modifier Applied
Your activity level adjusts your clinical risk score. Active individuals see up to 30% risk reduction. Sedentary patterns increase it by up to 30%.
⚡
📄
Step 04
Your Personalised Report
You receive a downloadable PDF report - formatted like a clinical diagnostic report - with your risk score, AHA category, key metrics and actionable recommendations to share with your doctor.

Where We're Going

Our Vision

Heart risk prediction for everyone
We envision a world where no heart attack is a surprise. Personalised cardiovascular risk assessment should be as easy as checking your steps - accessible, instant and free.
More wearables, more insight
We plan to expand beyond Google Fit - integrating Apple Health, Fitbit and Samsung Health - so every patient's lifestyle data can inform their risk prediction.
